QUICK START

Your first session, step by step.

Follow this sequence before starting a larger publishing run.

01

Download & open

Download PagePilot.exe and put it in a writable folder, such as Documents\PagePilot. Open it with internet connected.

02

Activate your license

Paste the key supplied by your owner and press Activate. If you need a key, enter your name and send the device request to your supplier.

03

Set up your profiles

In Profiles, import or create your own profiles. Check their login state, then use Scan selected Pages. Select only Pages you manage.

04

Choose your media

Open Media Library and scan your content folder. Prepare the media and captions you want to publish.

05

Prepare & test a post

Choose Reels, Videos or Stories. Review the selected profile, Page, media and caption. Start with a small test before a large queue.

06

Monitor & update

Watch Publish Queue and Activity Log. Resolve review or blocked jobs before retrying. In Updates, wait until an update is ready, then close and reopen to apply it.

A few things to know.

Do I need a license?

Yes. Get a key from your supplier. An ordinary unbound key binds to the first machine that successfully activates it. A machine-reserved key works on the intended device.

Can I move to another computer?

Contact your supplier for a device reset before activating on another machine.

Will an update stop my current posting?

No forced restart is used. Verified releases download in the background and apply on the next normal GUI launch. Let posting finish before closing.

Is an available update already installed?

No. Check the installed version and the automatic download status. Once ready, close and reopen PagePilot. If an error remains, contact your supplier.

What does the device request send?

The activation request includes your hashed Device ID, software version and optional name. Facebook profiles are not sent in that device request.
